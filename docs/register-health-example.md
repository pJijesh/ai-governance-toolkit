## Windows used

- Risk review window: 90 days. Accepted risk window: 180 days.
- Incident window: 30 days. External reporting decision window: 14 days.

These are working defaults, not legal limits. Set your own with the command line options and record why.

## What this report cannot tell you

It checks dates, statuses and links between registers. It cannot tell you whether a rating is well judged, whether a mitigation works, or whether an incident has been reported when the law requires it. Take legal advice on reporting duties.
