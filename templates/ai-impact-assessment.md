# AI impact assessment

> General information only, not legal advice. Fernhill Building Supplies Ltd is a fictional company and every example in this file is invented.

Supports controls TK-RSK-03 (impact assessment), TK-RSK-04 (fundamental rights and data protection check), TK-RSK-05 (stakeholder engagement), TK-INV-03 (prohibited practice screening) and TK-USE-03 (information for affected people). Related framework items: ISO/IEC 42001 6.1.4, 8.4 and A.5.2 to A.5.5, EU AI Act Art 27 and Art 26(9), NIST AI RMF MAP 3.1, MAP 3.2 and MAP 5.1.

## Purpose

An impact assessment asks one question: who could be affected by this system, and how? It is a different exercise from a risk register entry. The register tracks risks to the organisation and its objectives. The impact assessment looks outward at people and groups, including people who never chose to use the system.

## When to do one

- Before a new AI system goes live, or before a pilot that touches real people.
- When a system is changed in a way that affects its purpose, its data or its users.
- At least once a year for any system in a high-risk tier.
- When an incident suggests the original assessment missed something.

Low-risk systems can use the short screening in section 1 and stop if the answers are all no.

## Relationship to other assessments

- A data protection impact assessment (DPIA) is separate and often required where personal data is processed. Link to it from section 7 and reuse its facts. Do not duplicate it.
- A fundamental rights impact assessment under Art 27 of the Act applies only to a defined group of deployers, such as public bodies and private bodies providing public services, and deployers of certain credit and insurance systems. Check Art 27(1) with your legal adviser. Where it applies, sections 4 and 5 give you a starting structure but do not replace the required content.

## Worked example: TalentLens CV shortlisting (fictional)

System FH-AI-002, Fernhill Building Supplies Ltd. Fictional content.

### 0. Details

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Assessment reference | FH-IA-002 |
| System ID | FH-AI-002 |
| System name | TalentLens CV shortlisting |
| Assessment owner | HR Director |
| Contributors | Recruitment lead, Data protection officer, Legal or compliance lead, Head of IT, one branch manager |
| Date | 2026-08-25 |
| Version | 1.1 |
| Trigger | New system (pilot) |

### 1. Screening

| Question | Yes / No |
|---|---|
| Influences a decision about a person | Yes |
| Processes personal data | Yes |
| Used with children or vulnerable people | No |
| Annex III area or banned practice | Yes (Annex III point 4(a), recruitment and selection) |
| Direct interaction or generated content | No |

### 2. Purpose and context

- Intended purpose: rank incoming applications for branch and warehouse roles against the role profile so that recruiters can reach a shortlist faster.
- Users: five recruiters and HR assistants.
- Affected: external job applicants, mainly in the UK.
- Current process: recruiters read every application by hand. Peak campaigns produce several hundred applications per role.
- Why AI: reduce time to shortlist during peak campaigns. The pilot will test whether the saving is real and whether shortlist quality is at least as good.

### 3. Prohibited practice check

| Practice considered | Conclusion | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Inferring emotions of people in the workplace (Art 5) | Not used. The supplier's optional video interview analysis is disabled | Contract schedule 3 and configuration screenshot |
| Biometric categorisation or social scoring | Not used | Supplier documentation reviewed |

### 4. Impact analysis

| Affected group | Possible benefit | Possible harm | Likelihood | Severity | Reversible? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Applicants generally | Faster response | Wrongly ranked low and never reviewed | 3 | 4 | Yes, if reviewed by a person |
| Applicants with career breaks | None specific | Lower ranking from patterns in past hiring data | 3 | 5 | Partly |
| Applicants with disabilities | Standard process | Format of CV or gaps penalised | 2 | 5 | Partly |
| Recruiters | Less repetitive work | Over-reliance on the ranking | 4 | 4 | Yes |
| Fernhill | Faster hiring | Legal and reputational harm from unfair outcomes | 2 | 4 | Partly |

### 5. Mitigations and residual impact

| Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Date | Residual level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unfair ranking | Supplier bias testing results requested; Fernhill compares pilot outcomes by group; no automatic rejection | HR Director | 2026-12-11 | Medium |
| Over-reliance | Recruiter records reason for following or departing from the ranking; training on automation bias | HR Director | 2026-11-27 | Medium |
| Disability | Reasonable adjustment route published; any applicant can ask for a manual review | Recruitment lead | 2026-11-20 | Low |
| Missing documentation | Instructions for use and logging details requested before pilot ends | Procurement lead | 2027-01-15 | Medium |

Related risk register entries: FH-R-005 to FH-R-009.

### 6. Transparency and redress

- Notice on the careers page and in the application acknowledgement stating that software supports shortlisting and that a person makes the decision.
- Any applicant can ask for a manual review through the recruitment mailbox. Requests are logged and answered by a recruiter who did not see the original ranking.
- The staff forum was briefed on the pilot before launch.

### 7. Links

- DPIA reference: FH-DPIA-014
- Supplier review reference: FH-VR-003
- System card reference: FH-SC-002
- Human oversight checklist reference: FH-HO-002

### 8. Decision and sign-off

| Decision | Approve with conditions |
|---|---|
| Conditions | Pilot limited to UK roles and to branch and warehouse roles. Pause and review if the group comparison shows unexplained differences. Re-assess before any wider rollout |
| Approver | Managing Director |
| Date | 2026-09-08 |
| Next review date | 2027-01-15 |

Note: in October 2026 an incident (FH-INC-003) showed that applicants with career breaks were ranked lower in one requisition. The pilot for that role was paused. The assessment was reopened. This is recorded in the [incident log example](../examples/fernhill/ai-incident-log.csv).
